Defining regional water

  1. Create terrain and dry shores in FAF Map Editor, leave original water enabled and save your map.
  2. Open the .scmap file here, then use Load regions to load an existing .regionalwater file.
  3. Choose + Polygon and click around your lake. Press Enter, click the first point, double-click the last point or use Finish. Backspace removes the last point; Esc cancels.
  4. Set water height on the right. Drag vertices or edit X/Z. Double-click an edge to add a point. Drag inside to move the region.
  5. Keep region boundaries in dry terrain above both adjacent water levels. Polygons must not cross or touch themselves. Use 3–32 points and up to eight separate regions.
  6. Click Save regions and place the downloaded file alongside the map as yourmap.scmap.regionalwater, without (1) or .txt.
  7. Place the complete map folder in TestGame/Maps and run the experimental Regional Water engine v0.6 or later.

Islands are terrain above the water level. Polygon regions preserve existing terrain. Red boundaries indicate insufficient dry separation. The preview is approximate; verify water, pathfinding and gameplay in the game. Ctrl+Z: undo; Ctrl+Y: redo; Ctrl+S: save.

Works offline; the SCMAP is never overwritten. Versions 56/60 up to 4096 × 4096 are supported. Unsaved work is not retained when closing.
